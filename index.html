FAQ

Before you reach out

Answers to the questions that usually come up before a project starts.

How many revisions do I get?

Every package includes a set number of revision rounds on the chosen concept (usually 2, more on larger packages). Small tweaks — colour, spacing, copy swaps — don't count against that. Extra rounds beyond what's included are billed at a simple hourly add-on.

What file formats do I receive?

You get print-ready files (AI/EPS/PDF as needed) plus web-ready exports (PNG, JPG, SVG) sized for their intended use. Source files are included so you're never locked out of your own brand.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — happy to sign a standard NDA before we discuss anything confidential, whether that's your own template or a short one I can send over.

What if I don't like the first concept?

That's what the revision rounds are for. Tell me specifically what's missing or off, and we adjust the direction from there. If the concept is fundamentally the wrong fit, we'll reset with a fresh direction rather than force it.

How do payments work?

Typically a deposit upfront to lock in the timeline, with the balance due on final delivery. For larger, multi-part projects this can be split into milestones instead.

Can you work with an existing brand or style guide?

Absolutely — if you already have brand guidelines, fonts or colours, I'll design within them rather than starting from scratch. Just share what you have during the discovery step.
